---
<p><strong>Stay alert!</strong></p><p>Be careful if someone other than your coordinator contacts you on behalf of Alimat Group, or if you receive suspicious-looking correspondence. If you are not sure whether the person contacting you really represents Alimat Group, contact our nearest office.</p><p><strong>Fraud attempts misusing the Alimat Group brand</strong></p><p>Please be aware that people with no connection to Alimat Group increasingly pretend to be part of the Alimat Group brand. Without our consent, they:</p><ul><li>pose as Alimat Group recruiters and contact potential candidates by email, letter, phone or social media;</li><li>encourage potential candidates to get in touch by publishing fake job ads that unlawfully use the Alimat Group brand.</li></ul><p>Scammers approach their “potential victims” and ask them for personal data or for payments. Remember that no Alimat Group brand ever asks candidates taking part in a recruitment process for payment or for a copy of a credit or debit card.</p><p>If you learn of any such misuse of the Alimat Group brand, in particular of fraud attempts, please report it to us as soon as possible.</p>
