<h2>Which documents do you need to submit quickly?</h2>
<p>Once you find a new job, you must apply to have your residence decision changed. <strong>Without doubt</strong>, you will need Annex No. 1 (załącznik nr 1) for this. This document must be filled in correctly by your new employer. <strong>In addition</strong>, you will need a new employment contract or civil-law contract. In some cases, the office will also ask for a new labour market test.</p>
<h2>Do you always need a new residence card?</h2>
<p>Many candidates wonder how <strong>changing employers with a residence card</strong> works in official practice. <strong>Well</strong>, the plastic card itself stays in your wallet. Only the voivode’s paper decision is replaced. <strong>On the other hand</strong>, if you had a card without access to the labour market, the whole procedure is different.</p>
<h2>Avoid these mistakes when changing jobs!</h2>
<p><strong>Firstly</strong>, never delay sending the notification to the office.
