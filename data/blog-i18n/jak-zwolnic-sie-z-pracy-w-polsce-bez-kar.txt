<p>The rules for leaving depend mainly on the document you signed. <h2>Why walking out of a job is a huge mistake</h2>
<p>Never leave your job from one day to the next. This kind of sudden disappearance is commonly called abandoning the job. Unfortunately, dishonest agencies often impose huge financial penalties for it. What is more, you may lose your pay for the last month you worked entirely. This behaviour also seriously damages your reputation on the job market.</p>
<h2>How to hand in your resignation properly</h2>
<p>Simply telling your boss that you are leaving is definitely not enough. You must always hand in an official letter on an ordinary sheet of paper.
